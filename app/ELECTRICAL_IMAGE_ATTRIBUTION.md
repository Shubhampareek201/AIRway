## Reuse requirements

- Preserve author attribution and a link to the source page and licence wherever these
  assets are displayed or documented in a distributed product.
- CC BY-SA assets require adaptations to be shared under the same or a compatible
  licence. Do not remove these attribution records.
- These are title-matched source candidates. Confirm their appearance in the AIRway
  customer app on a real device and verify the Commons redirect/network behaviour before
  marking the catalogue image audit as visually approved.
- Admin-uploaded service/gallery images continue to take priority over these fallbacks.
